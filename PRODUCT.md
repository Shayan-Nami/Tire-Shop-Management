# Product scope

## Platform

web

## Users

Persian-speaking tire-shop managers, warehouse staff and technicians. Desktop and mobile are equally important.

## Product Purpose

Keep shop sales, service lines, customers/vehicles, inventory movements, purchases and settlement connected, with clear screens for frequent daily operations.

## Operating Context

Frequent transactional work on computers and touch devices, in a Persian RTL interface with English/LTR support for interface copy and locale-aware formatting. Light and dark themes are existing capabilities.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The application serves one shop, with role-based access, invoice printing, stock updates on confirmation and settlement records. It uses React/TypeScript, Tailwind and Lucide. Appointments, bank transactions, multi-tenant hosting and general-ledger accounting are outside its scope.

## Product Principles

- Keep amounts, quantities and document states easy to distinguish.
- Make frequent actions easy to find on both keyboard and touch screens.
- Preserve the meaning and availability of existing workflows.
- Use consistent controls and clear feedback across every screen.
